Privacy Policy

Apk Share: Bluetooth & WiFi (shown in the app as “Wifi Share”) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short
  • Your files are shared directly between devices. We never upload them to our servers – we do not run servers that receive your content.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), usage statistics and crash reports.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised only with your consent, which you can change at any time in the app menu → Ad privacy settings.
  • No account is needed, and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Android app “Apk Share: Bluetooth & WiFi” (package com.luckydeveloper.apkshare), published on Google Play by its independent developer (“we”, “us”). Contact: seyxsultan@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The following is processed only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Files and media you browse, manage or share – photos, videos, music, documents and apps.
  • Contacts – only when you move to a new phone, sync with your PC, share a contact in chat or save a contact from a QR code. Contacts go only to the device or computer you choose.
  • Installed apps and app usage – for the app manager, app sharing and app-insights tools.
  • On-device analysis – grouping photos by face, text recognition, image labelling, document scanning and translation (Google ML Kit and on-device models), and heart-rate readings from the camera. Face data and readings are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Chat – messages, voice messages and chat history are stored on your device and sent only to the people in your chat room.
  • Transfer history and settings.

3. Sharing between devices

Files, messages and game moves travel directly between devices over Wi-Fi Direct, a hotspot, Bluetooth, your local network or Google Nearby Connections – never through our servers. Whoever you connect with receives what you send, so connect only with people you trust. Web Share and PC Sync make the files you pick available to browsers on your local network while they are running, and casting sends the media you pick to the TV you choose.

5. Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, and you can revoke any permission in Android settings.

  • Location and nearby devices – Android requires them to find nearby devices over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. We do not collect or store your location.
  • Storage, media and all-files access – to browse, share and manage your files.
  • Camera – QR codes, document scanning and the heart-rate tool.
  • Microphone – voice messages, screen recording with sound, the sound meter and amplifier, and sound pairing; used only while you use one of these features.
  • Contacts – phone migration, PC sync and sharing contacts.
  • Installed apps, usage access and app installation – the app manager, app insights and installing apps you receive.
  • Notifications – transfer progress.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Personalised advertising relies on your consent, collected through Google's consent form. Crash reporting and app analytics rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. Purchases are processed to provide what you bought. Where the law requires consent for any processing, we ask for it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): change or withdraw it at any time in the app menu → Ad privacy settings.
  • Personalised ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads).
  • No ads: the “Remove Ads” in-app purchase turns ads off.
  • Permissions: revoke them at any time in Android settings.
  • Data on your device: delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use these rights.

8. Data retention and deletion

We do not run servers that store your data. Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Google keeps analytics, crash and advertising data for limited periods under its own retention policies. To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your device, email us.

9. International transfers

Google, ipinfo.io and Cloudflare may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can help remove it.

11. Security

Transfers happen directly over your local connection. Use trusted networks and connect only with devices you know. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
